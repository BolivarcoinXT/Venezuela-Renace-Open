# VENEZUELA RENACE

## INDEPENDENT CITIZEN ADDENDUM VR-001

### Protection of the Venezuelan People, Recovery of National Wealth and Mandatory Preconditions for Public Debt Restructuring

**Version:** 1.0 — Draft for Public Review  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Status:** Independent Citizen Proposal  
**Reference Document:** Venezuela Renace — National Program for Recovery and Prosperity, November 2026.

---

## 1. PURPOSE AND GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

The protection of Venezuelan citizens, their economic well-being, their national wealth and the prosperity of future generations shall take absolute precedence over the financial interests of creditors, government officials, financial intermediaries and speculative investors.

The Venezuelan people shall not be required to bear the financial consequences of corruption, misappropriation of public funds, illicit enrichment, irresponsible public borrowing, fraudulent financial transactions or predatory debt practices without first exhausting all available mechanisms for financial accountability and asset recovery.

The Venezuelan State shall have a mandatory obligation to identify, investigate, trace, freeze, confiscate and recover, through lawful procedures, public assets misappropriated or unlawfully transferred within or outside the national territory.

The recovery of national wealth shall take precedence over the repayment, refinancing or restructuring of historical public debt.

No government administration shall transfer the financial burden of unrecovered public assets to Venezuelan citizens through increased taxation, inflationary financing, reductions in essential public services or additional indebtedness as a substitute for fulfilling its asset-recovery obligations.

**Only after all mandatory conditions established in this addendum have been fully satisfied, all available lawful asset-recovery mechanisms have been exhausted, and the results have been independently audited, certified and publicly disclosed, may the Venezuelan people consider the restructuring or repayment of the remaining verified and legally enforceable public debt.**

These conditions are absolute, mandatory and non-negotiable.

---

## 2. CONDITION A — MANDATORY FINANCIAL AUDIT OF ALL PUBLIC EMPLOYEES AND THEIR IMMEDIATE FAMILIES

### A.1. Universal audit requirement

Every individual who has held a position, performed official duties or received employment compensation from the Venezuelan State or any state-owned enterprise or entity during the preceding 25 years shall be subject to a mandatory, comprehensive and independent financial audit.

This requirement shall extend to immediate family members, including spouses, domestic partners, children, parents and siblings.

### A.2. Scope of the financial audit

The comprehensive audit shall include:

- Personal income tax returns.
- Domestic and international bank accounts.
- Real estate and other personal assets.
- Corporate ownership and financial interests in foreign jurisdictions.
- Investment accounts and financial securities.
- Digital assets and cryptocurrency holdings.
- Venezuelan sovereign and PDVSA bond holdings.
- Trusts, foundations and beneficial ownership interests.
- Sources of income and accumulated wealth.
- Transfers, gifts and asset transactions between family members.
- Identification of unexplained increases in personal wealth.
- Assets held through nominees, intermediaries or third parties.

### A.3. Mandatory and non-negotiable compliance

No exemptions, waivers, selective audits, statistical sampling or political agreements shall substitute for the comprehensive financial audit of every individual covered by this provision.

No distinction shall be made based on political affiliation, military rank, government position, employment level, retirement status, nationality or current country of residence.

Resignation, retirement, relocation abroad or the transfer of assets to family members or third parties shall not exempt any individual from the corresponding financial investigation.

### A.4. Voluntary participation and expedited auditing program

The Venezuelan State may establish a voluntary early-participation program to accelerate the auditing process by encouraging individuals to proactively submit financial records, disclose assets and cooperate with independent auditors.

Voluntary participation shall not reduce the scope, quality, depth, independence or evidentiary standards of the audit.

Participation in this program shall not constitute automatic financial clearance, immunity from judicial proceedings, forgiveness of financial misconduct or exemption from asset-recovery proceedings.

The voluntary nature of the program refers exclusively to early cooperation.

**The audit shall remain mandatory for every individual covered by this condition.**

### A.5. Restrictions on government employment and public contracts

Until an individual has successfully completed the mandatory financial audit and received independent certification of compliance, that individual shall be ineligible to:

- Hold or continue holding any government position.
- Receive employment compensation from government institutions or state-owned enterprises.
- Enter into, renew or benefit from government contracts.
- Participate in public procurement processes.
- Receive public contracts indirectly through corporations, partnerships, intermediaries or beneficial ownership arrangements.
- Serve as a director, executive, adviser or representative of a state-owned enterprise.

These restrictions shall apply to all individuals covered by this condition, including immediate family members, subject to applicable legal procedures and due-process protections.

No provisional authorization, political appointment or administrative decision shall substitute for successful completion of the audit.

### A.6. Government responsibility and administrative capacity

**No argument claiming that the audit is too difficult, too expensive, too time-consuming, administratively complex or beyond the operational capacity of the State shall be accepted as justification for waiving, reducing or abandoning this requirement.**

The Venezuelan State shall establish the institutional, technological, financial and human resources necessary to fulfill this obligation, including obtaining international assistance where necessary.

Administrative limitations shall be treated as problems to be resolved, not as excuses for noncompliance.

### A.7. Independent oversight and enforcement

All audits shall be conducted under independent supervision and in accordance with applicable legal procedures.

Indications and evidence of illicit enrichment, misappropriation of public funds, money laundering or concealment of assets shall be referred to the competent judicial authorities for appropriate legal action and asset recovery.

Until 100% of the required financial audits have been completed and independently certified, no historical public debt payment, refinancing, restructuring agreement or binding repayment commitment shall be authorized.

**Condition A is absolute, mandatory and non-negotiable.**

## 3. CONDITION B — WORLDWIDE RECOVERY OF VENEZUELAN PUBLIC GOLD

### B.1. International investigation

The Venezuelan State shall undertake a comprehensive and independent international investigation to identify, locate, trace and recover all Venezuelan public gold reserves unlawfully transferred, concealed, misappropriated or retained outside the country.

This requirement shall include Switzerland and any other country or jurisdiction where the financial audits established under Condition A, or subsequent investigations, identify Venezuelan public gold holdings subject to lawful restitution.

### B.2. Scope of investigation

The investigation shall include gold held through:

- Foreign central banks and commercial banks.
- Private financial institutions and vaults.
- Corporations, trusts and foundations.
- Current and former public officials and their family members.
- Nominees, intermediaries and third parties.
- Any other domestic or international custody arrangements.

The State shall establish the original ownership, quantity, destination, custody arrangements and subsequent transactions involving such gold.

### B.3. Mandatory recovery

The recovery of 100% of independently verified Venezuelan public gold subject to lawful restitution shall be mandatory.

The State shall pursue all available legal, diplomatic and international cooperation mechanisms necessary to secure its recovery.

Administrative difficulties, jurisdictional complexity, financial costs, diplomatic obstacles, foreign government resistance or the passage of time shall not constitute acceptable grounds for abandoning, reducing or waiving this requirement.

All recovered gold shall be independently verified, valued, audited and recorded as Venezuelan public assets.

The results shall be publicly disclosed.

### B.4. Absolute prohibition on debt payments and refinancing

Until the gold recovery requirement has been fully satisfied and independently certified, the Venezuelan State shall not authorize:

- Principal payments on historical public debt.
- Interest payments.
- Debt refinancing.
- Debt restructuring agreements.
- Debt exchanges or settlements.
- New financial instruments intended to replace, refinance or service historical debt obligations.
- Direct or indirect financial commitments intended to circumvent this condition.

Partial recovery, promises of future restitution or ongoing negotiations shall not constitute sufficient compliance.

The geographical scope of this requirement shall be worldwide and shall not be limited to Switzerland.

**NO GOLD RECOVERY, NO PAYMENT, REFINANCING OR RESTRUCTURING OF HISTORICAL PUBLIC DEBT.**

**Condition B is absolute, mandatory and non-negotiable.**

---

## 4. CONDITION C — MINIMUM RECOVERY OF 50% OF MISAPPROPRIATED PUBLIC WEALTH

### C.1. Independent determination of corruption-related losses

An independent financial investigation shall establish the total documented value of Venezuelan public wealth misappropriated through corruption, fraudulent public contracts, illicit enrichment, money laundering, unauthorized transfers and other unlawful financial activities.

The investigation shall encompass the preceding 25 years and shall include all relevant government institutions and state-owned enterprises.

### C.2. Minimum recovery threshold

At least 50% of the independently verified value of misappropriated public wealth shall be recovered before historical public debt becomes eligible for consideration for restructuring or repayment.

This percentage constitutes a mandatory minimum requirement and shall not represent a limitation on subsequent recovery efforts.

The State shall continue pursuing the recovery of all remaining assets.

### C.3. Asset-recovery accounting standards

The State shall maintain an independently audited public asset-recovery ledger identifying:

- Total verified value of misappropriated public wealth.
- Assets identified and located.
- Assets frozen or subject to judicial proceedings.
- Assets legally confiscated.
- Assets actually recovered.
- Recovery-related expenses.
- Value of assets remaining unrecovered.
- Independently certified recovery percentages.

Frozen assets, pending claims and promises of restitution shall not be classified as assets actually recovered.

Duplicate claims and overlapping asset valuations shall be eliminated.

### C.4. Protection against manipulation of figures

No government institution shall artificially reduce the documented value of misappropriated public wealth for the purpose of facilitating compliance with the minimum recovery threshold.

Any modification of the established financial baseline shall require independent verification, documented justification and public disclosure.

**Recovery of at least 50% constitutes a mandatory minimum, not a negotiable objective or a justification for abandoning further recovery efforts.**

## 5. CONDITION D — COMPREHENSIVE NATIONAL AND INTERNATIONAL ASSET-RECOVERY PROGRAM

The Venezuelan State shall establish a permanent and independent National Public Asset Recovery Authority.

This authority shall possess the legal powers and institutional, financial, technological and investigative resources necessary to identify and recover Venezuelan public wealth in any jurisdiction worldwide.

Its responsibilities shall include:

- Independent forensic investigations.
- Preservation of financial records and evidence.
- International asset tracing.
- Beneficial ownership investigations.
- Cooperation with foreign governments and financial institutions.
- Judicial proceedings for asset confiscation and restitution.
- Protection of whistleblowers and witnesses.
- Independent accounting of recovered assets.
- Periodic publication of recovery results.

The authority shall not be controlled exclusively by government institutions or individuals subject to its investigations.

Recovered assets shall be deposited into separately maintained, independently audited public accounts and protected against subsequent diversion or misappropriation.

The program shall operate in accordance with applicable international asset-recovery frameworks, including Chapter V of the United Nations Convention against Corruption.

**Historical public debt restructuring shall not commence until this program is fully established, operational, adequately funded and subject to independent supervision.**

---

## 6. CONDITION E — COMPREHENSIVE FINANCIAL AND LEGAL VERIFICATION OF PUBLIC DEBT

The approximately USD 237.54 billion in public-sector obligations identified in the Venezuela Renace report shall constitute a preliminary financial inventory and shall not, by itself, represent automatic recognition of legally enforceable obligations.

Every debt claim shall undergo independent financial and legal verification.

The verification shall include:

- Original loan agreements and bond issuance documentation.
- Constitutional and legislative authorization.
- Original principal amounts.
- Evidence of actual receipt of borrowed funds.
- Destination and utilization of borrowed funds.
- Interest calculations and applicable contractual terms.
- Previous payments and settlements.
- Guarantees and pledged assets.
- Potential fraud, corruption and conflicts of interest.
- Applicable statutes of limitation and legal defenses.
- Identification of duplicate obligations and obligations between public-sector entities.

No disputed, unsupported, fraudulent or legally invalid claim shall be automatically incorporated into a debt restructuring agreement.

Disputed claims shall be resolved through applicable legal procedures.

**Only independently verified and legally enforceable obligations shall qualify for consideration for eventual repayment or restructuring.**

---

## 7. CONDITION F — CREDITOR TRANSPARENCY AND IDENTIFICATION OF BENEFICIAL OWNERSHIP

Every creditor seeking payment, refinancing or participation in a public debt restructuring process shall be required to establish its legal entitlement and disclose the identity of its ultimate beneficial owners to the designated independent authority.

The verification shall include ownership structures, intermediaries, investment funds, entities established in foreign jurisdictions and other arrangements through which financial benefits may be received.

Particular attention shall be given to potential connections between historical public debt and:

- Current and former Venezuelan public officials.
- Immediate family members of public officials.
- Individuals or entities implicated in corruption investigations.
- Misappropriated Venezuelan public funds.
- Money laundering and illicit enrichment.
- Fraudulent financial transactions.
- Undisclosed conflicts of interest.

Where legally obtainable, acquisition records, purchase prices and sources of funds used shall be independently examined.

Purchasing distressed debt at prices below its face value shall not, by itself, constitute evidence of unlawful activity.

Evidence of criminal misconduct shall be referred to the competent judicial authorities.

**No creditor shall receive payment without first establishing its lawful entitlement to receive such payment.**

## 8. CONDITION G — BLOCKCHAIN-BASED PUBLIC TRANSPARENCY AND INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION

### G.1. Mandatory public transparency infrastructure

The Venezuelan State shall establish a publicly accessible, independently verifiable blockchain-based system for recording, authenticating and monitoring financial information associated with the implementation of this addendum.

The system shall utilize open-source software, publicly verifiable infrastructure and independent validation mechanisms.

No government administration, financial institution or private technology provider shall possess the unilateral ability to alter or delete historical records without such alterations being detectable.

### G.2. Public financial records

The system shall provide permanent, tamper-evident and independently verifiable records of:

- Financial audit completion and certification.
- Identified, frozen, confiscated and recovered public assets.
- Recovered gold inventories and custody verification.
- Amounts recovered from corruption-related activities and recovery percentages.
- Verified public debt obligations.
- Government revenues and expenditures.
- Public payroll expenditures and aggregate audit results.
- Public contracts and procurement awards.
- State-owned enterprise financial statements.
- Sovereign debt negotiations and final agreements.
- National asset valuations and transfers.
- Allocation and utilization of recovered public funds.
- Principal and interest payments on public debt.

### G.3. Public access and independent verification

All Venezuelan citizens, independent auditors, journalists, academic institutions and international observers shall have free access to public financial records and the tools necessary to independently verify their authenticity.

Public records shall be available in accessible, downloadable and machine-readable formats.

Verification of public information shall not depend exclusively on authorization, infrastructure or systems controlled by the government of the day.

### G.4. Protection of confidential information

Personal tax returns, private banking records and other legally protected information shall remain securely confidential.

Cryptographic proofs, digital signatures and independent audit certifications shall enable verification of the integrity of such records without unnecessarily exposing personal information.

Sensitive personal information shall not be published directly onto immutable public blockchain infrastructure.

### G.5. Prohibition against secret financial agreements

All proposed historical public debt restructuring agreements shall be publicly disclosed before final authorization, including:

- Principal and interest obligations.
- Repayment schedules.
- Guarantees and pledged assets.
- Financial intermediary fees.
- Consulting and legal expenses.
- Commissions.
- Material financial commitments.
- Estimated financial consequences for Venezuelan citizens.

No undisclosed side agreements shall be permitted.

### G.6. Mandatory compliance

No asset-recovery certification or historical public debt payment shall be considered compliant with this addendum unless the required supporting information and verification proofs have been published through the designated transparency system.

Technological complexity, implementation costs, administrative limitations or institutional incapacity shall not constitute grounds for abandoning this requirement.

**Public access, independent verification and the integrity of historical financial records are mandatory and non-negotiable requirements.**

---

## 9. CONDITION H — PROTECTION OF STRATEGIC NATIONAL ASSETS

Venezuelan strategic public assets shall be protected against unauthorized transfers, artificially reduced valuations, fraudulent privatizations and predatory collateral arrangements.

This protection shall extend to:

- PDVSA and its subsidiaries.
- CITGO and other foreign corporate holdings.
- Central Bank of Venezuela gold reserves.
- International monetary reserves.
- Mineral rights.
- Public infrastructure.
- Future oil and natural gas revenues.
- Other strategically significant public assets.

Any proposed privatization, ownership transfer, concession, collateral arrangement or disposition of strategic assets shall require independent valuation, public disclosure and applicable constitutional authorization.

No government institution shall circumvent the debt-payment restrictions established in this addendum by transferring public assets, granting preferential economic rights or pledging future national revenues to satisfy historical obligations.

---

## 10. CONDITION I — CITIZEN PARTICIPATION AND FINAL AUTHORIZATION

Only after independent certification of compliance with all mandatory conditions established in this addendum may a historical public debt repayment or restructuring proposal be presented to the Venezuelan people for consideration.

The State shall publish a comprehensive final financial reconciliation identifying:

- Original reported public debt.
- Independently verified debt.
- Invalidated, disputed and duplicated claims.
- Recovered gold and other public assets.
- Wealth recovered from corruption-related activities.
- Remaining legally enforceable debt balance.
- Proposed repayment terms.
- Estimated annual debt-service obligations.
- Expected financial consequences for Venezuelan citizens.

Any proposed agreement shall demonstrate that repayment obligations will not compromise essential public services, economic recovery or the fundamental rights of Venezuelan citizens.

Final authorization shall require meaningful citizen participation and compliance with applicable constitutional approval procedures.

A national referendum may be established as an additional mechanism for citizen authorization.

**The Venezuelan people shall have the opportunity to evaluate the remaining legitimate debt balance before assuming new binding financial commitments.**

---

## 11. PROHIBITION AGAINST CIRCUMVENTION

Until all mandatory conditions established in this addendum have been fulfilled, the State shall not authorize:

- Principal payments on historical public debt.
- Interest payments on historical public debt.
- Debt refinancing or restructuring.
- Debt exchanges or settlements.
- New financial instruments intended to replace historical debt.
- Transfers of public assets intended to satisfy historical obligations.
- Indirect payments through state-owned enterprises or intermediaries.
- Collateral arrangements intended to circumvent these restrictions.
- Binding agreements acknowledging or renewing unverified financial obligations.

Preliminary discussions exclusively intended to preserve legal defenses, prevent asset seizures, suspend litigation, limit accumulating interest, verify creditor claims or facilitate asset recovery may be permitted, provided such discussions do not authorize repayment, refinancing or binding debt restructuring agreements.

No political agreement, emergency declaration, creditor demand, international financial pressure or administrative justification shall constitute grounds for waiving these requirements.

---

## 12. INDEPENDENT CERTIFICATION AND ENFORCEMENT

Compliance with each condition shall be independently verified and certified by legally authorized institutions possessing sufficient operational independence from government and creditor interests.

Certification shall be supported by documentary evidence and publicly verifiable financial records.

False certifications, concealment of assets, destruction of evidence, manipulation of financial records and unauthorized financial commitments shall be investigated and referred to the competent judicial authorities.

No government institution shall be permitted to certify its own compliance without independent verification.

The fulfillment of one condition shall not substitute for the fulfillment of another.

All available lawful asset-recovery mechanisms shall be exhausted before final consideration of historical public debt restructuring.

---

## 13. FINAL DECLARATION

The Venezuelan people shall not be treated as the financial guarantors of corruption, illicit enrichment, fraudulent borrowing or predatory financial arrangements.

Venezuela's economic recovery shall be founded upon financial accountability, recovery of national wealth, institutional transparency, independent verification and the protection of its citizens.

The recognition, repayment, refinancing or restructuring of historical public debt shall remain conditional upon the fulfillment of all mandatory requirements established in this addendum.

Only the remaining verified and legally enforceable debt balance shall be eligible for consideration after all required investigations, audits and asset-recovery efforts have been completed and independently certified.

**FIRST: FINANCIAL ACCOUNTABILITY.**

**SECOND: RECOVERY OF NATIONAL WEALTH.**

**THIRD: RESTRUCTURING OF THE REMAINING LEGITIMATE DEBT.**

The protection of the Venezuelan people is the governing purpose of this addendum.

**THIS PRINCIPLE IS ABSOLUTE, MANDATORY AND NON-NEGOTIABLE.**
